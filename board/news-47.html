HAI Lab Presented at HCI Korea 2023

2023.03.02







국립 서울과학기술대학교 인간중심 인공지능 연구실 (HAI Lab) 서경원 교수, 김유원 석박통합과정, 김도현 석사과정, 박보겸 석박통합과정, 박민영 학부연구원(위에서 부터 순서대로)이 한국HCI 2023 학회에서 아래 주제로 구두 발표 하였습니다.

Kyoungwon Seo (Assistant Professor), Yuwon Kim (Integrated Ph.D. student), Dohyun Kim (M.S. student), Bogyeom Park (Integrated Ph.D. student), Minyoung Park (Undergrad researcher) presented the research below at the HCI KOREA 2023 conference.


- Digital Marker through AI Analysis Technology in the Metaverse (Kyoungwon Seo)

- Virtual Kiosk Test for Early Screening of Mild Cognitive Impairment through Eye Tracking Data Analysis (Se Young Kim)

- Deep Learning Model for Early Screening of Patients with Alzheimer's Disease and Mild Cognitive Impairment using EEG-SSVEP (Dohyun Kim)

- Counterfactual vs. Prefactual: Different Impacts of Two Narrative AIs on Causability for Complex Health Data (Hyobin Park)

- Passive vs. Active: Which Behavior is Better for Predicting At-Risk Students in On line Learning? (Minyoung Park)
