Donggyu Yeo, Yun Ji Noh, Jin Kim Complete First AURA Program

2022.02.21



국립 서울과학기술대학교 인간중심 인공지능 연구실 (HAI Lab) AURA (AI Undergrad Research Assistant) 프로그램 1기 수료를 축하합니다.

- 여동규 연구원 (인공지능응용학과 2학년) ▶ 연구주제: Virtual Reality Education Environment Design Strategies

- 노윤지 연구원 (인공지능응용학과 2학년) ▶ 연구주제: AI chatbot for Language Education

- 김진 연구원 (기계자동차공학과 4학년) ▶ 연구주제: Time-series data analysis

We are celebrating the successful completion of the first AURA program at SeoulTech HAI Lab.

- Donggyu Yeo (Applied Artificial Intelligence, 2nd year) ▶ Research topic: Virtual Reality Education Environment Design Strategies

- Yun Ji Noh (Applied Artificial Intelligence, 2nd year) ▶ Research topic: AI chatbot for Language Education

- Jin Kim (Mechanical and Automotive Engineering, 4th year) ▶ Research topic: Time-series data analysis
