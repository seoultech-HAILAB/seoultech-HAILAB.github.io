HAI Lab 2026 Recruitment: Call for Undergraduate Researchers

2026.03.31

The Human-centered AI Lab (HAI Lab, advisor: Prof. Kyoungwon Seo) in the Department of Applied Artificial Intelligence is recruiting undergraduate researchers. We study AI that understands and augments human behavior, cognition, and emotion in real-world settings such as healthcare, education, and manufacturing, using AI, Human-Computer Interaction, and AR/VR.

1. Eligibility — SeoulTech undergraduates in their 3rd or 4th year (as of the 2026 academic year); students interested in AI and research; students considering graduate school.

2. Benefits — a scholarship; a dedicated desk and research equipment (PC, GPU server, iPad, etc.; Sangsang Hall #410); opportunities to attend domestic and international conferences (e.g., ACM CHI), publish in top SCI journals, and file patents; joint research with partner institutions (Asan Medical Center, Hyundai Motor Company, UBC in Canada, etc.).

3. Research areas and activities — Biomedical AI, AI for Education, Agentic AI, Vision-Language-Action models, and Immersive XR + AI. Undergraduate researchers join ongoing lab projects (LLM-based agents, AI model development) and take part in paper reviews and seminars.

4. Schedule — Applications: April 1 (Wed) – April 12 (Sun), 2026, 12:00 PM. Document screening results: April 20 (Mon), 2026, 12:00 PM (individual notice). Interviews: scheduled individually with applicants who pass screening.

5. How to apply — Application form: https://forms.gle/vwkdidKAWbBVZm8u8. Inquiries: Bogyeom Park, Ph.D. student, HAI Lab (bogyeom@seoultech.ac.kr).

인공지능응용학과 인간중심 인공지능 연구실(지도교수: 서경원)에서 AI, Human-Computer Interaction, AR/VR 기술을 활용해 의료, 교육, 제조 등 실제 현장 속 인간의 행동·인지·감정을 이해하고 증강하는 AI 기술을 연구할 학부연구원을 모집합니다.

1. 신청자격 및 우대사항

- 서울과학기술대학교 학부 3-4학년 재학생 (2026학년도 기준)

- 인공지능 및 연구 활동에 관심이 있는 학생

- 대학원 진학을 고려 중인 학생

2. 학부연구원 혜택

- 소정의 장학금 지원

- 연구실 전용 좌석 및 연구 장비 지원 (PC, GPU 서버, iPad 등 / 상상관 410호)

- 국내/국제학회(예: ACM CHI 등) 참석, SCI급 탑 저널 작성, 특허등록 기회

- 국내외 기관과의 공동연구 기회 (서울아산병원, 현대자동차, 캐나다 UBC 등)

- 최신 연구 논문 리뷰 및 세미나 참여
