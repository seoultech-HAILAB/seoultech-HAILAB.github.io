Prof. Kyoungwon Seo Publishes Paper in ETHE (SSCI, Q1, Top 2%)

2021.11.25

국립 서울과학기술대학교 인간중심 인공지능 연구실 (HAI Lab) 서경원 교수가 캐나다 The University of British Columbia와 이스라엘 Technion 연구팀과의 국제공동연구를 International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education (SSCI, Education & Educational Research 분야 상위 2%)에 논문 게재하였습니다.

- 제목: The impact of artificial intelligence on learner–instructor interaction in online learning

- 링크: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s41239-021-00292-9

Prof. Kyoungwon Seo collaborated with the University of British Columbia (Canada) and the Technion (Israel) to publish a joint international research article in the International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education (SSCI, Q1 Top 2% in Education & Educational Research).

- Title: The impact of artificial intelligence on learner–instructor interaction in online learning

- Link: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s41239-021-00292-9
