Bogyeom Park & Yuwon Kim Publish Paper in JMIR (SCIE, Q1, Top 3%)

2024.04.21

국립 서울과학기술대학교 인간중심 인공지능 연구실 (HAI Lab) 박보겸, 김유원 석박사통합과정 연구원이 한양대병원 연구팀과의 공동연구를 Journal of Medical Internet Research (SCIE, Health Care Sciences & Services 분야 상위 3%)에 논문 게재하였습니다.

- 제목: Integrating Biomarkers From Virtual Reality and Magnetic Resonance Imaging for the Early Detection of Mild Cognitive Impairment Using a Multimodal Learning Approach: Validation Study

- 링크: https://www.jmir.org/2024/1/e54538

Bogyeom Park & Yuwon Kim (Integrated Ph.D. Students) published a joint research article with the Hanyang University Hospital (Korea) in the Journal of Medical Internet Research (SCIE; top 3% in Health Care Sciences & Services).


- Title: Integrating Biomarkers From Virtual Reality and Magnetic Resonance Imaging for the Early Detection of Mild Cognitive Impairment Using a Multimodal Learning Approach: Validation Study

- Link: https://www.jmir.org/2024/1/e54538
