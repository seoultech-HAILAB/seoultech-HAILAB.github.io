Prof. Kyoungwon Seo Publishes Paper in ETHE (SSCI, Q1, Top 2%)

2023.06.30

국립 서울과학기술대학교 인간중심 인공지능 연구실 (HAI Lab) 서경원 교수가 이스라엘 Technion, 국립 한밭대학교, 경일대학교 연구팀과의 국제공동연구를 International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education (SSCI, Education & Educational Research 분야 상위 2%)에 논문 게재하였습니다.

- 제목: Supporting students’ self-regulated learning in on line learning using artificial intelligence applications

- 링크: https://educationaltechnologyjournal.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s41239-023-00406-5

Prof. Kyoungwon Seo collaborated with the Technion (Israel), Hanbat National University (Korea), and Kyungil University (Korea) to publish a joint international research article in the International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education (SSCI, Q1, Top 2% in Education & Educational Research).

- Title: Supporting students’ self-regulated learning in on line learning using artificial intelligence applications

- Link: https://educationaltechnologyjournal.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s41239-023-00406-5
