Self-Disclosing Chatbot

The chatbot opens up first

Ask students about their worries and you get a word or two. So this chatbot shares its own worries first, then asks.

Minyoung Park · Bogyeom Park · Kyoungwon Seo

›Try It

›Video

›Notes

  • Stress assessed from these conversations agreed with the standard SISCO inventory at 0.94 within ±1 point. The control chatbot without self-disclosure: 0.86.
  • The only difference between the two chatbots is self-disclosure. The questionnaire is identical.
  • Students who talked with this chatbot named their stressors more concretely and made more actionable coping plans.
  • A demonstration, not a screening tool. No one is diagnosed or assessed here.
