Virtual Kiosk Test

One burger order screens for cognitive decline

Hold the order in your head, hunt through an unfamiliar menu, press at the right moment. With this single ordering task, the study screened patients with mild cognitive impairment at 93.3% accuracy.

Yuwon Kim · Bogyeom Park · Kyoungwon Seo

›Try It

›Video

›Notes

  • 20 healthy older adults and 31 patients with MCI took this task. Hand movement speed · fixation ratio · completion time · errors — all four markers separated the two groups.
  • Hand and gaze need a VR controller and an eye tracker. This browser demo measures only completion time and errors.
  • The test takes 5–15 minutes. Adding MRI biomarkers lifts classification accuracy to 94.4%.
  • These screens are the interface used in the validation study. A demonstration, not a screening test.
